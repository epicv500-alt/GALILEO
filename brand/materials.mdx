Every material in a Galileo product was chosen after asking the same question: does this belong here, or did we choose it because it was easy? The answers shaped a lineup built almost entirely from natural, honest materials — some ancient, some engineered, all selected because they perform better over time than the alternatives we rejected.

## Natural Rubber

Natural rubber is harvested as latex from the *Hevea brasiliensis* tree — tapped by hand, collected, and processed without the petrochemical feedstocks required to manufacture synthetic rubber. The result is a material that is supple, grippy, and responsive in a way synthetic alternatives rarely match.

**Why Galileo uses it:** For **The Mat**, grip and surface feel are not secondary concerns — they are the entire point. Natural rubber holds its traction through sweat and pressure without off-gassing, and it does not break down the way foam alternatives do under daily use. It also compresses and recovers honestly, without the memory loss that afflicts cheaper mat materials over time.

<Info>
  Natural rubber is biodegradable. When The Mat eventually reaches the end of its life — which is designed to be years from now — the rubber base does not persist indefinitely in landfill the way synthetic foam does.
</Info>

## Sustainably Harvested Cork

Cork is the outer bark of the cork oak (*Quercus suber*), harvested by stripping the bark without cutting down the tree. A single cork oak can be harvested every nine years for over a century. No tree is felled; no forest is cleared.

**Why Galileo uses it:** Cork brings a natural, tactile warmth to the surface of **The Mat** that rubber alone cannot provide. It is naturally antimicrobial, meaning the surface resists odor and bacteria without chemical treatment. It is also genuinely beautiful — each piece carries the natural grain and variation of the tree it came from, which means no two mats are identical.

<Tip>
  The cork surface of The Mat will develop a subtle patina with use. This is not wear — it is character. The more consistently you practice on it, the more the surface settles into your routine.
</Tip>

## Undyed Cotton

The hand-stitched edge of **The Mat** is finished in undyed cotton — unbleached, unprocessed, and sewn by hand. Cotton in its natural state is a warm off-white, and that is the color of the thread used here.

**Why Galileo uses it:** The edge stitch on The Mat is functional before it is decorative. It seals the layers together and defines the perimeter of the mat. Undyed cotton was chosen over colored or synthetic alternatives because it avoids the dye chemicals that most finishing threads carry, and because its natural warmth complements the cork surface without competing with it.

## Stainless Steel (Double-Walled)

**The Vessel** is constructed from double-walled stainless steel — two concentric walls with a vacuum-sealed gap between them. The result is thermal insulation that keeps cold drinks cold and hot drinks hot for hours, without condensation on the outer surface.

**Why Galileo uses it:** Stainless steel is one of the most durable materials available for a vessel carried daily. It does not leach flavor or chemicals into contents, it does not dent easily, and it does not degrade with repeated washing. Double-walled construction eliminates the condensation that makes single-wall vessels uncomfortable to hold and damaging to surfaces.

<Note>
  The brushed matte finish of The Vessel is not a coating — it is a surface treatment applied directly to the steel. This means it will not chip or peel. Minor surface marks from daily carry blend into the finish naturally over time.
</Note>

## Brass

The collar of **The Vessel** — the ring where the lid meets the body — is finished in a brass tone. Brass is a copper-zinc alloy with a warm, golden character that contrasts deliberately against the cool matte of the steel body.

**Why Galileo uses it:** The brass collar is the one moment of warmth in The Vessel's otherwise utilitarian form. It is also practical: the collar provides a positive grip point when opening the lid, and its weight gives the action a satisfying solidity. Brass does not corrode under normal use, and the tone it develops with handling — a subtle deepening of warmth — improves rather than diminishes the object.

## Brushed Aluminum

**The Case** is machined from solid aluminum billet and finished with a fine brushed matte treatment. There are no visible seams; the form is essentially one continuous piece.

**Why Galileo uses it:** Aluminum offers an exceptional strength-to-weight ratio — rigid enough to protect its contents, light enough to carry without thought. The solid construction means there are no weak points at joins or hinges. The brushed surface treatment diffuses light evenly and hides the minor surface contact that comes with a carried object.

## Soft-Touch Microfiber

The interior of **The Case** is lined in soft-touch microfiber — an ultra-fine synthetic fiber with a texture that is closer to suede than to fabric. It is the one synthetic material in the Galileo line.

**Why Galileo uses it:** The lining of The Case must protect whatever is placed inside it from the aluminum walls. No natural fiber provides the consistent, non-abrasive surface that microfiber does at this scale. The soft-touch finish was selected specifically because it does not catch on smooth surfaces or leave lint. It is the right material for the application, which is why it is here despite being synthetic.

## Cold-Pressed Oat & Oat Milk

**The Bar** is a cold-processed soap made with cold-pressed oat and oat milk as its primary skin-conditioning ingredients. Cold pressing preserves the oils and proteins in the oat grain that heat processing would destroy.

**Why Galileo uses it:** Oat has one of the best-documented track records in skin care for gentleness and soothing effect — particularly for dry or sensitive skin. Using cold-pressed oat and oat milk rather than synthetic emollients or fragrance oils keeps the formula simple, effective, and free from ingredients that commonly cause irritation. The Bar contains no sulfates and no synthetic fragrance, which means it is suitable for daily use on face and body.

<Tip>
  The Bar is unscented by design. Most soap fragrance comes from synthetic compounds or added essential oils that can irritate sensitive skin. The mild, natural scent of The Bar is the scent of the oat itself — clean, faintly cereal, and gone quickly.
</Tip>

## Magnesium Flake

The foundation of each variety in **The Soak** is magnesium flake — a high-purity, mineral-derived form of magnesium chloride sourced from ancient seabeds. It dissolves cleanly in warm water without residue.

**Why Galileo uses it:** Magnesium chloride is absorbed transdermally — through the skin — during a bath. It is used to support muscle relaxation and general recovery, which makes it a functional rather than decorative ingredient. Unlike Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate), magnesium flake dissolves more completely and leaves less residue in the tub.

## Dried Botanicals

Each variety of **The Soak** includes dried botanicals — air-dried, unprocessed plant material selected for its visual presence in the water and its natural aromatic character.

**Why Galileo uses it:** The botanicals in The Soak are not there to simulate a fragrance; they are there to provide it naturally. As they steep in warm bath water, they release their oils and scent slowly and gently — nothing synthetic, nothing concentrated beyond what the plant itself contains. The visual effect of botanicals dispersing in water is also, we will admit, its own kind of pleasure.

<Info>
  The specific botanicals vary by Soak variety. All are air-dried and unprocessed — no synthetic coatings, no color preservation treatments. They will continue to fade naturally over time if stored in light, which is why we recommend keeping The Soak in a cool, dark place.
</Info>

## Start Shopping

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a collection">
    From the homepage, select **Apparel** or **Accessories** to enter that section of the shop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and add to your bag">
    Tap any product to see full details, then click **Add to Bag**. Your bag appears in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your bag">
    Open your bag at any time to adjust quantities, remove items, or proceed to checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your order">
    Enter your shipping details and confirm your order. Complimentary shipping is included on every order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

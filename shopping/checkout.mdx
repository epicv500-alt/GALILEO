The Galileo checkout page is designed to be straightforward and transparent. Before you commit to a purchase, you'll see a complete breakdown of everything in your order on the left, while a clean shipping details form on the right collects only what's needed to get your items to you. Every order ships complimentarily — no codes, no minimums.

## The Checkout Page at a Glance

The checkout page is divided into two columns:

- **Left column — Order Summary:** lists each item, the subtotal, the shipping cost, and your order total
- **Right column — Shipping Details:** a form where you enter your delivery information and place your order

## Reviewing Your Order Summary

Before entering any personal details, take a moment to confirm your order looks exactly right.

The Order Summary shows:

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Items** | Each product in your bag with its name and price |
| **Subtotal** | The combined cost of all items |
| **Shipping** | Complimentary |
| **Total** | The final amount you will be charged |

<Info>
  Galileo includes complimentary shipping on every order automatically. No promo code is required, and there is no minimum spend.
</Info>

## Entering Your Shipping Details

The **Shipping Details** form is on the right side of the checkout page. Fill in each field carefully — this information determines where your order is delivered and where your confirmation email is sent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your Full Name">
    Type your first and last name exactly as you'd like them to appear on your shipping label.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Email address">
    Provide a valid email address. Your order confirmation will be sent here, so double-check the spelling before moving on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Address">
    Type your full street address, including any apartment or unit number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your City">
    Enter the city for your delivery address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Postal Code">
    Provide the postal or ZIP code for your delivery address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Take an extra moment to verify your email address. It's the only way you'll receive your order confirmation, and a typo means you won't be able to look up your order details later.
</Tip>

## Placing Your Order

Once your shipping details are complete and your order summary looks correct, you're ready to confirm.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review everything one final time">
    Scan both the Order Summary and the Shipping Details form to make sure all information is accurate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Place Order">
    Click the **Place Order** button at the bottom of the Shipping Details form to submit your order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Clicking **Place Order** confirms and submits your purchase. Make sure your shipping address and email are correct before proceeding — changes cannot be made once an order is placed.
</Warning>

## After You Place Your Order

As soon as your order is confirmed, you'll be taken to a Thank You confirmation screen that acknowledges your purchase. A confirmation email will also be sent to the address you provided in the Shipping Details form.

For full details on what happens next, see [Your Galileo Order Confirmation: What to Expect](/shopping/order-confirmation).

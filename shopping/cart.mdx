Your Galileo Bag is a sliding cart drawer that keeps your selected items close at hand without pulling you away from browsing. Once you've entered a collection and added something you love, the **Bag** button appears in the top-right corner of every collection and product page — giving you a quick, uninterrupted path from discovery to purchase.

## Opening Your Bag

The Bag button is intentionally hidden on the Galileo landing page to keep the welcome experience clean and focused. As soon as you navigate into any collection, the button becomes visible in the top-right corner of the page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse to a collection">
    From the Galileo home page, select any collection to enter the product browsing experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot the Bag button">
    Look for the **Bag** button in the top-right corner of the page header. It appears once you're inside a collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Bag to open the drawer">
    Click the **Bag** button to slide open your cart drawer from the right side of the screen. Your saved items appear immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Bag button is not visible on the Galileo landing page. Navigate into any collection first, and the button will appear in the header.
</Note>

## What Your Bag Shows

Every item in your bag is displayed with enough detail to keep your choices clear at a glance.

Each line item includes:

- **Color swatch thumbnail** — a small preview of the exact color variant you selected
- **Product name** — the full name of the item
- **Price** — the individual unit price
- **Quantity controls** — **−** and **+** buttons to decrease or increase the quantity
- **Remove button (×)** — removes the item from your bag entirely

At the bottom of the drawer, the **subtotal** reflects the combined cost of all items currently in your bag.

## Adjusting Quantities

You can change how many units of any item you want directly from the bag drawer — no need to return to the product page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Bag">
    Click the **Bag** button in the top-right corner to open the drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the item to adjust">
    Locate the product whose quantity you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the + or − buttons">
    Click **+** to add one more unit, or **−** to reduce the quantity by one. The subtotal updates automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The subtotal at the bottom of the drawer recalculates instantly each time you adjust a quantity, so you always see an up-to-date total before heading to checkout.
</Tip>

## Removing Items

If you change your mind about a product, you can remove it from your bag with a single click.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Bag">
    Click the **Bag** button to open the cart drawer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the × button">
    Click the **×** button on the right side of the item you want to remove. The item is immediately cleared from your bag and the subtotal updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing Your Subtotal

The bag footer always displays your current **subtotal** — the sum of every item in your bag at their listed prices. This figure does not yet include any checkout-specific details; the full order breakdown, including shipping, appears on the checkout page.

<Info>
  All Galileo orders include complimentary shipping. You'll see the shipping line reflected as **Complimentary** when you proceed to checkout.
</Info>

## Proceeding to Checkout

When you're happy with the contents of your bag, you're one click away from checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your bag">
    Confirm the items, quantities, and subtotal shown in the drawer are correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Checkout">
    Click the **Checkout** button in the bag footer. You'll be taken to the checkout page where you can review your full order summary and enter your shipping details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Placing an order with Galileo triggers two confirmations: an immediate on-screen message that lets you know your order went through, and a follow-up email sent to the address you provided at checkout. Together, these give you everything you need to verify your purchase and keep a record of it.

## The On-Screen Thank You Confirmation

The moment your order is successfully submitted, Galileo replaces the checkout page with a confirmation screen. You don't need to do anything to trigger it — it appears automatically after you click **Place Order**.

The confirmation screen displays:

> **Thank You — Your order has been confirmed. A confirmation will be sent to your email shortly.**

A **Continue Shopping** button is also shown, letting you return to the Galileo collections whenever you're ready.

<Note>
  The on-screen confirmation appears immediately and means your order has been received. The email confirmation follows shortly after and serves as your permanent record of the purchase.
</Note>

## Your Confirmation Email

A confirmation email is sent to the email address you entered in the Shipping Details form at checkout. This email is your official record of the transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Open the inbox for the email address you used at checkout. The confirmation typically arrives within a few minutes of placing your order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look for the Galileo confirmation email">
    The email is sent from Galileo and will reference your order in the subject line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your order details">
    Open the email and verify that all the details match what you intended to order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Confirmation Email Contains

Your confirmation email includes the key details of your order so you have a complete record in one place:

- **Order confirmation** — acknowledgment that your order was received and is being processed
- **Items ordered** — the products you purchased
- **Shipping address** — the delivery address you entered at checkout
- **Complimentary shipping** — a note confirming that shipping is included at no charge

<Info>
  All Galileo orders ship complimentarily. Your confirmation email will reflect a shipping cost of Complimentary, so there are no surprise charges to watch for.
</Info>

## If Your Confirmation Email Doesn't Arrive

If you don't see the confirmation email within a few minutes of placing your order, there are a few quick steps to take before reaching out for help.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait a few minutes">
    Email delivery can occasionally take up to 10 minutes. If the email hasn't arrived yet, wait a little longer before troubleshooting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your spam or junk folder">
    Confirmation emails can sometimes be filtered automatically. Open your spam or junk folder and search for any message from Galileo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the email address you used">
    If possible, return to the checkout and confirm that the email address you entered was spelled correctly. A single typo can send your confirmation to the wrong address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark Galileo as a trusted sender">
    If the email landed in spam, mark it as "Not Spam" or add the Galileo sender address to your contacts to ensure future emails arrive in your inbox.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Search your entire mailbox — including all folders — for the word "Galileo" or "order confirmed" to surface any misfiled confirmation emails quickly.
</Tip>

## Still Haven't Received Your Confirmation?

If you've checked your spam folder, confirmed your email address is correct, and still haven't received a confirmation after 15–20 minutes, contact Galileo support. When you reach out, have the following ready to speed up the process:

- The email address you used at checkout
- The approximate date and time you placed your order
- The items you ordered

<Warning>
  Do not place a duplicate order before confirming with support that your original order was not received. The on-screen Thank You confirmation is a reliable indicator that your order went through successfully.
</Warning>

## Continuing to Shop

Once you've reviewed your confirmation, click the **Continue Shopping** button on the Thank You screen — or navigate back to any Galileo collection — to keep browsing.

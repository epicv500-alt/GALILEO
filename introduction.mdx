## Who Galileo Is For

Galileo is for people who are done settling. Whether you practice yoga every morning, prioritize clean personal care, or simply want fewer, better things on your shelf, Galileo's collection is curated to meet you there. The brand appeals to those who value provenance, prefer natural materials over synthetic alternatives, and believe that good design is a form of self-respect.

## What to Expect When You Shop

Shopping at Galileo is intentionally simple. The experience opens with a clean split-screen landing page that invites you to choose your focus — Apparel or Accessories. From there, you browse a tightly edited product listing, explore individual product detail pages, and add items to a sliding cart drawer before checking out with a straightforward form.

<Note>
  Shipping is complimentary on every Galileo order, with no minimum purchase required.
</Note>

There are no membership tiers, no points programs, and no friction. Just a well-made product, a clear price, and a smooth path to your door.

## The Galileo Standard

Every material is chosen for a reason. Natural rubber and cork for grip and sustainability. Stainless steel for longevity. Aluminum for precision. Cold-processed oats for gentleness. These are not marketing words — they are the actual answers to the question "why this material?" that Galileo asks of every product before it earns a place in the collection.

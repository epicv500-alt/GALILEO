## Your Morning Routine

The morning is about cleansing gently and preparing your skin for the day. The Bar's oat-based formula is unscented and sulfate-free, making it a straightforward first step before anything else you apply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Splash with warm water">
    Before reaching for The Bar, splash your face with warm water to prepare the skin surface. Warm — not hot — is the right temperature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lather The Bar in your hands">
    Rub The Bar between wet palms to build a light lather. You don't need much — the dense cold-processed formula is concentrated and goes a long way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cleanse your face and neck">
    Apply the lather to your face and neck using gentle circular motions. Rinse thoroughly with cool or lukewarm water.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pat dry">
    Pat — don't rub — your skin dry with a clean towel. Follow with whatever moisturiser or SPF is part of your morning practice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The Bar is suitable for both face and body — use it head to toe in your morning shower to keep your routine streamlined and your product count low.
</Tip>

## Your Evening Routine

In the evening, the goal shifts to cleansing away the day's buildup. The Bar's sulfate-free, unscented formula makes it a gentle choice for a nightly cleanse.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rinse with warm water first">
    If you've been wearing sunscreen or makeup, a quick rinse with warm water before using The Bar helps the lather work more effectively.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Double-cleanse when needed">
    On days with heavier coverage or sunscreen, work a lather of The Bar across your face once, rinse, and lather a second time. The sulfate-free formula is gentle enough for this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse thoroughly">
    Rinse until the water runs completely clear. Pat your face dry before applying any follow-on products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your evening skincare">
    Follow with a serum, oil, or balm if that's part of your routine. The Bar leaves your skin clean and ready for whatever comes next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Weekly Ritual: The Soak

Once a week, set aside twenty to thirty minutes for a bath with The Soak. This is a longer, more intentional practice — a chance to slow down and enjoy the mineral soak.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your evening">
    The Soak works best at the end of a day when you have time to slow down. Sunday evenings, or any evening before a rest day, are a natural fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw a warm bath">
    Fill your bath with comfortably warm water — aim for a temperature you can step into easily. Very hot water can degrade the botanicals before they fully release.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one sachet as the bath fills">
    Drop a single sachet into the water while it's still running. The movement of the water disperses the magnesium flake and botanicals more evenly than adding it to still water.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it steep for two to three minutes">
    Before you get in, allow the sachet a couple of minutes to begin releasing. The magnesium dissolves quickly; the dried botanicals infuse gradually throughout the soak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soak for twenty to thirty minutes">
    Settle in and rest. Twenty to thirty minutes is a comfortable duration for the full experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pat dry and rest">
    After your bath, pat your skin dry gently. Follow with a body oil or balm if you like, then rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Each set of The Soak contains three sachets. Used weekly, one set carries you through three weeks of the ritual.
</Tip>

## Your Morning Routine

The morning is about waking your skin up gently and preparing it for the day. The Bar's oat-based formula cleanses without stripping, which makes it ideal as a first step before anything else you apply.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Splash with warm water">
    Before reaching for The Bar, splash your face with warm water to open pores slightly and prepare the skin surface. Warm — not hot — is the right temperature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lather The Bar in your hands">
    Rub The Bar between wet palms to build a light lather. You don't need much — the dense cold-processed formula is concentrated and goes a long way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cleanse your face and neck">
    Apply the lather to your face and neck using upward circular motions. The oat milk in The Bar supports your skin barrier while the lather lifts away overnight buildup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse and pat dry">
    Rinse thoroughly with cool or lukewarm water, then pat — don't rub — your skin dry with a clean towel. Follow with whatever moisturiser or SPF is part of your morning practice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The Bar is suitable for both face and body — use it head to toe in your morning shower to keep your routine streamlined and your product count low.
</Tip>

## Your Evening Routine

In the evening, the goal shifts from preparation to restoration. Your skin repairs itself overnight, and a gentle cleanse with The Bar removes the day's buildup without compromising that process.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cleanse with warm water first">
    If you've been wearing sunscreen or makeup, a quick rinse with warm water before using The Bar helps the lather work more effectively.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Double-cleanse when needed">
    On days with heavier coverage or sunscreen, work a lather of The Bar across your face once, rinse, and lather a second time. The sulfate-free formula is gentle enough for this without drying your skin out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse thoroughly">
    Rinse until the water runs completely clear. Any remaining soap residue can sit on the skin overnight and cause irritation — especially for sensitive skin types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your evening skincare">
    Pat your face dry and follow with a serum, oil, or balm if that's part of your routine. The Bar leaves the skin clean without disrupting its natural moisture balance, so your subsequent products can absorb effectively.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your skin feels tight after cleansing, it's usually a sign the water temperature was too hot or the rinse wasn't thorough enough — not that the cleanser is too harsh. The Bar's formula is specifically designed not to over-strip.
</Note>

## Your Weekly Ritual: The Soak

Once a week, set aside twenty to thirty minutes for a bath with The Soak. This is a longer, more intentional practice — less about cleansing and more about rest, recovery, and giving your skin the benefit of a full mineral soak.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your evening">
    The Soak works best at the end of a day when you have time to slow down. Sunday evenings, or any evening before a rest day, are a natural fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draw a warm bath">
    Fill your bath with comfortably warm water — aim for a temperature you can step into easily. Overly hot water breaks down the botanicals faster and can be taxing on skin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one sachet as the bath fills">
    Drop a single sachet into the water while it's still running. The movement of the water disperses the magnesium flake and botanicals more evenly than adding it to still water.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it steep for two to three minutes">
    Before you get in, allow the sachet a couple of minutes to begin releasing. The magnesium dissolves quickly; the dried botanicals infuse gradually throughout the soak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soak for twenty to thirty minutes">
    Settle in and rest. Magnesium is most effective when absorbed transdermally over time — there's no need to rush this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pat dry and rest">
    After your bath, pat your skin dry gently. The magnesium leaves a soft residue that continues to absorb — you don't need to rinse it off. Follow with a body oil or balm if you like, then rest.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Each set of The Soak contains three sachets. Used weekly, one set carries you through three weeks of the ritual — long enough to feel a genuine difference in how your skin and body recover.
</Tip>

## Tips for Sensitive Skin

Galileo's skincare products are formulated with sensitivity in mind. If your skin reacts easily to fragrance, sulphates, or synthetic additives, both The Bar and The Soak are well-suited to your routine.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

<Card title="Unscented" icon="wind">
The Bar contains no added fragrance — synthetic or natural. Fragrance is one of the most common triggers for skin sensitivity, and leaving it out entirely was a deliberate choice.
</Card>

<Card title="Sulfate-Free" icon="shield-check">
Sulfates create a rich lather but can strip the skin's natural oils. The Bar lathers gently without them, leaving the skin clean without the tight, dry feeling that sulphate-based cleansers can cause.
</Card>

<Card title="Oat-Based Formula" icon="leaf">
Cold-pressed oat and oat milk are among the most well-studied ingredients for sensitive skin. They support the skin barrier, calm irritation, and help maintain moisture balance with every wash.
</Card>

<Card title="Mineral Botanicals" icon="sparkles">
The Soak's dried botanicals are selected to complement the magnesium flake — nothing in the formula is there for appearance alone. If you have known botanical sensitivities, check the ingredient list before your first use.
</Card>

</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If you're introducing The Bar to a new skincare routine, start with once-daily use for the first week and observe how your skin responds before moving to morning and evening cleansing. Most skin types adapt immediately, but a gradual introduction is always a good approach.
</Note>

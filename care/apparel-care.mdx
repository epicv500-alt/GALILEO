## The Mat

Cork is naturally antimicrobial and surprisingly resilient, but it rewards gentle handling. After every session, a quick wipe-down keeps the surface fresh and extends the life of the stitching.

### Cleaning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wipe after every use">
    Use a damp cloth — plain water is usually enough. For a deeper clean, add a small drop of mild, natural soap to the cloth before wiping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it air dry">
    Lay The Mat flat or hang it over a rail in a shaded, well-ventilated space. Allow it to dry completely before rolling it up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot-clean the cotton edge">
    If the hand-stitched cotton border needs attention, use a barely damp cloth and blot gently. Avoid saturating the stitching.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Storage

Roll The Mat loosely with the **cork side facing out**. This relieves pressure on the rubber base and prevents the cork surface from cracking over time. Store it upright or flat in a cool, dry place.

<Tip>
  A cotton or canvas strap is the ideal way to carry and store The Mat — it allows the material to breathe while keeping the roll secure.
</Tip>

### What to Avoid

<Warning>
  Do not use harsh chemical cleaners, bleach-based sprays, or alcohol on The Mat. These will degrade the cork surface and weaken the natural rubber base.
</Warning>

- **Prolonged sun exposure** — UV light dries out cork and can cause the rubber to break down. Keep The Mat out of direct sunlight when not in use.
- **Soaking in water** — submerging The Mat will compromise the rubber base and loosen the edge stitching over time.
- **Rolling cork side in** — always roll with the cork facing outward to protect the surface and maintain the mat's natural shape.

### Extending Its Life

The cork top will develop a gentle patina with regular use — this is normal and part of what makes each mat unique. If the surface ever feels less grippy, a light wipe with a damp cloth (no soap) just before practice can restore traction quickly.

## The Vessel

The hand-brushed matte finish and brass-toned collar are what set The Vessel apart, and both reward a little care. Hand washing keeps both elements looking their best over the long term.

### Cleaning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rinse after every use">
    Empty The Vessel and rinse thoroughly with warm water immediately after use, especially if you've had anything other than water in it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand wash with mild soap">
    Use a small amount of mild dish soap and a soft bottle brush to clean the interior. Wash the collar cap separately by hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse thoroughly">
    Make sure no soap residue remains inside — rinse with clean water several times and leave the cap off to air dry completely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Storage

Store The Vessel upright with the cap off when not in use. This allows airflow through the interior and prevents any moisture from becoming trapped — the most common cause of odour in insulated bottles.

<Tip>
  If The Vessel develops an odour, fill it with warm water and a tablespoon of baking soda, leave it overnight, then rinse thoroughly the next morning.
</Tip>

### What to Avoid

<Warning>
  The Vessel is not dishwasher safe. The heat and detergents used in dishwashers will damage the hand-brushed matte finish and can discolour the brass-toned collar over time.
</Warning>

- **Freezing** — do not place The Vessel in a freezer. Expansion from freezing liquids can stress the double-wall seal.
- **Carbonated beverages** — the pressure from carbonation can affect the cap seal and alter the finish of the collar over time.
- **Abrasive sponges** — use only soft cloths or bottle brushes on both the exterior and interior to preserve the brushed finish.

### Extending Its Life

The brass-toned collar can be gently polished with a dry microfiber cloth to maintain its warmth and sheen. Avoid letting moisture sit on the collar for long periods — dry it off after washing and before storing.

## The Case

The Case is precision-milled from a single block of aluminum and finished with a soft-touch microfiber lining inside. Its beauty is in its restraint — keeping it clean is straightforward.

### Cleaning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wipe the exterior">
    Use a dry cloth or a very slightly damp cloth to wipe down the aluminum exterior. The milled surface is smooth enough that dust and fingerprints lift away easily.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot-clean the lining">
    If the microfiber interior needs attention, use a dry or barely damp soft cloth and blot gently. Work in small sections and avoid rubbing vigorously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dry completely">
    Before placing your items back inside, make sure both the interior and exterior are fully dry. Leave the case open in a dry space for a few minutes after any cleaning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Storage

Keep The Case in a bag pocket, desk drawer, or any space where it won't be pressing against rough or sharp surfaces. The engraved line mark on the exterior is intentionally subtle — it stays that way with simple, mindful storage.

<Tip>
  Pairing The Case with a soft pouch is the best way to protect the aluminum finish when it's travelling alongside keys or other hard objects.
</Tip>

### What to Avoid

<Warning>
  Avoid placing The Case against abrasive surfaces — keys, coins, and rough textures can scratch the aluminum exterior over time.
</Warning>

- **Excessive moisture** — while a slightly damp cloth is fine for cleaning, avoid submerging or running water over The Case. Moisture in the microfiber lining takes time to dry.
- **Harsh solvents or sprays** — these can lift the microfiber lining and dull the aluminum finish. Plain water or a barely damp cloth is all you need.
- **Forcing the case open or closed** — The Case is precision-fitted. Open and close it with a steady, even motion to preserve the alignment of the hinge over time.

### Extending Its Life

Aluminum develops a natural patina with age and handling — slight variations in sheen are part of the material's character, not flaws. A dry microfiber cloth buff every so often is all it takes to keep the surface looking intentional and clean.

## The Bar

The Bar is cold-processed slowly and cured for six full weeks before it reaches you. That process creates a dense, long-lasting bar — but like all natural soap, it benefits most from one simple habit: letting it dry between uses.

### How to Use

The Bar is suitable for both face and body. Its unscented, sulfate-free formula makes it gentle enough for daily use on all skin types, including sensitive skin. Work it into a lather with wet hands and rinse thoroughly with warm water.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wet your hands or skin">
    Run warm water over your hands or the area you're cleansing. A small amount of water goes a long way with a dense cold-processed bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lather gently">
    Rub The Bar between your palms or directly onto skin in slow, circular motions until you have a rich lather. A little goes further than you expect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rinse thoroughly">
    Rinse with warm water until no residue remains. Pat dry with a clean towel — avoid rubbing, especially on the face.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return the bar to its dish">
    Place The Bar on a draining soap dish immediately after use so it can begin drying. This is the single most effective thing you can do to extend its life.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use The Bar on your face in the evening to gently remove the day. Its oat-based formula supports the skin barrier without stripping natural oils.
</Tip>

### Storage

A draining soap dish is essential. Any dish that allows water to run off — wood, ceramic with ridges, or a simple wire rack — will work well. The goal is to prevent The Bar from sitting in pooled water, which softens and dissolves natural soap much faster than regular use does.

<Warning>
  Avoid leaving The Bar in a soap dish that collects standing water. Prolonged contact with water will cause the bar to become mushy and deteriorate quickly, regardless of how long it was cured.
</Warning>

- Keep The Bar away from direct shower spray when not in use.
- If you have a second bar in reserve, store it unwrapped in a cool, dry place — a linen cupboard shelf works well.
- The bar's surface may develop slight variation in colour as it continues to cure in your home. This is normal and has no effect on performance.

## The Soak

Each set of The Soak contains three individual sachets, each one blended with magnesium flake and dried botanicals. They're designed to be used weekly as a restorative ritual — one sachet per bath.

### How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draw a warm bath">
    Fill your bath with comfortably warm water — not boiling. Very hot water can degrade the botanicals before they have a chance to release fully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add one sachet">
    Drop one sachet directly into the bath as the water runs. The movement of the water will help disperse the minerals and botanicals evenly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow a few minutes to steep">
    Before stepping in, let the sachet sit in the water for two to three minutes. The magnesium flake dissolves quickly; the botanicals release gradually throughout your soak.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Soak and rest">
    A soak of twenty to thirty minutes is ideal. After your bath, pat your skin dry gently — the magnesium leaves a soft residue that continues to absorb.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each set of The Soak contains three sachets — one for each week of a month, or spaced however suits your routine best.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The Soak is for external use only. Do not ingest the contents of any sachet.
</Warning>

### Storage

Store The Soak in a cool, dry place away from humidity. A bedroom shelf or a closed cabinet — away from the bathroom itself — is ideal. Moisture is the one thing that can cause the mineral content to clump or the botanicals to degrade before use.

- Keep sachets sealed until you're ready to use them.
- Do not store The Soak in a bathroom cabinet where steam from showers can reach it regularly.
- If gifting The Soak, keep the original packaging dry and sealed until it's given.

The Bar is a cold-processed soap made with cold-pressed oat and oat milk — ingredients that clean gently, support the skin barrier, and leave your skin feeling balanced rather than stripped. **$65**

The Soak is a set of three mineral-rich bath soaks, each blended with magnesium flake and dried botanicals. The botanicals vary across the three soaks to give each bath its own character without introducing synthetic fragrance. This is a ritual product — something you set aside time for.

Shopping at Galileo is designed to feel unhurried. The site guides you from a striking landing page through a curated product listing and into a streamlined checkout — every step intentional, every click purposeful. Here is exactly how to move through the experience.

## Step 1: Choose Your Collection

When you arrive at galileo.com, you are greeted by a full-screen split panel. The left side represents **Apparel**; the right side represents **Accessories**. Hover over either panel to see it animate and expand — a subtle invitation to explore.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Land on the homepage">
    The split-screen layout fills your viewport. Both panels are visible simultaneously so you can orient yourself before committing to a direction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover to preview">
    Move your cursor over either panel to trigger a smooth animation that brings the collection into focus. This works on any desktop or laptop browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click or press Enter to enter">
    Click the panel of your choice to navigate to that collection's product listing page. If you are navigating by keyboard, tab to the panel you want and press **Enter** to activate it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The split-screen landing page is fully accessible via keyboard navigation. Use **Tab** to move between the two panels and **Enter** to select one.
</Info>

## Step 2: Browse the Product Listing

Once inside a collection, you see all available products displayed in a clean grid. Each product card shows the product name, a primary image, and the price. Browse at your own pace — the listing is intentionally short, because Galileo only carries products it believes in.

  </Card>
  <Card title="Accessories" icon="sparkles" href="/products/accessories">
    **The Bar** — $48 · **The Soak** — $65 · **The Galileo Set** — $110

<Tip>
  You can navigate directly between Apparel and Accessories at any time using the site navigation — you do not need to return to the homepage split screen.
</Tip>

## Step 3: Open a Product Detail Page

Click any product card to open its detail page. Here you will find:

- A full product description and material callouts
- High-resolution imagery
- The product price
- An **Add to Bag** button

Read through the product details to confirm the item is right for you. Galileo's descriptions are written to give you everything you need to make a confident decision — no guesswork required.

## Step 4: Add an Item to Your Bag

When you are ready to purchase, click the **Add to Bag** button on the product detail page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add to Bag">
    The button is prominently placed on the product detail page. One click is all it takes — there are no size or variant selectors to navigate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the cart drawer open">
    A sliding cart drawer appears from the right side of the screen, showing your current bag contents, item prices, and a running total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue shopping or proceed">
    From the cart drawer, you can return to browsing by clicking anywhere outside it, or move forward by clicking **Checkout**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5: Review Your Bag

The sliding cart drawer is your running tally. It updates in real time each time you add a product. Review your selections here before heading to checkout.

<Note>
  Shipping is complimentary on all Galileo orders. The total shown in your cart drawer is the total you will pay — no shipping fees are added at checkout.
</Note>

## Step 6: Check Out

When your bag is ready, click **Checkout** inside the cart drawer. You will be taken to a simple, single-page checkout form. Fill in the following fields:

- **Full name**
- **Email address**
- **Shipping address**
- **City**
- **Postal code**

Once you submit your order, Galileo will send a confirmation to the email address you provided. Keep that email — it contains your order details and serves as your receipt.

<Warning>
  Double-check your email address before submitting. Your order confirmation and any follow-up correspondence will be sent there.
</Warning>

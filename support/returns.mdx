We want you to love everything you order from Galileo. If something isn't right, we make it straightforward to return — no frustration, no runaround. Our return policy is designed to be fair to you while preserving the quality standard every Galileo customer deserves.

## Return Eligibility

To qualify for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

- **Returned within 30 days** of the confirmed delivery date
- **Unused and unaltered** — the item has not been worn, washed, opened for use, or damaged after delivery
- **In its original packaging** — all tags, inserts, and protective materials must be intact

<Warning>
  Items that show signs of use, are missing original packaging, or are submitted after the 30-day window are not eligible for a return or refund.
</Warning>

### The Galileo Set

The Galileo Set ($110) must be returned as a complete set. Individual components of the set — The Bar and The Soak — cannot be returned separately if originally purchased as part of the set.

<Note>
  If you purchased The Bar ($48) or The Soak ($65) individually, standard return eligibility applies to each item on its own.
</Note>

## How to Initiate a Return

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email us within 30 days of delivery">
    Send a message to support@galileo.com with the subject line **Return Request — [Your Order Number]**. Include your name, order number, the item(s) you'd like to return, and a brief reason for the return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive return instructions">
    Our team will respond within 1–2 business days with a return authorization and detailed instructions for packaging and sending back your item.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack and ship your return">
    Repack the item in its original packaging, following the instructions provided. Ship the return using the method outlined in our response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await confirmation and refund">
    Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll send a confirmation email. Your refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Please allow **5–7 business days** after we confirm receipt of your return for the refund to appear, depending on your bank or card issuer's processing time.

<Info>
  Galileo does not charge restocking fees. Your refund reflects the full purchase price of the returned item(s).
</Info>

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, we'll make it right at no cost to you. Email support@galileo.com as soon as possible with your order number and a photo of the issue, and we'll prioritize a resolution.

## Contact Us

For any questions about returns, refunds, or your order status, reach out to our team at support@galileo.com. We're here to help.

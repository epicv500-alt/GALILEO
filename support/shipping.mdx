Every Galileo order ships complimentary — no minimum purchase required, no codes to enter, and no surprise fees at checkout. From the moment you place your order, we handle the details so your products arrive safely and beautifully.

## Order Confirmation

As soon as your order is placed, a confirmation appears on-screen and a copy is sent to your email address. This confirmation includes your order summary and serves as your receipt.

<Note>
  If you don't see your confirmation email within a few minutes, check your spam or promotions folder. You can also reach us at support@galileo.com and we'll resend it.
</Note>

## Estimated Delivery Times

Delivery windows are estimates and may vary depending on your location, carrier availability, and seasonal demand. All times are measured in business days from the date your order ships.

| Destination | Estimated Delivery |
|---|---|
| Domestic (US) | 3–5 business days |
| International | 7–14 business days |

<Info>
  Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Carrier delays or customs processing for international orders may extend these windows. We appreciate your patience.
</Info>

## Packaging

Galileo packaging is designed with the same care as the products inside. We use minimal, thoughtful materials — undyed where applicable — to reduce waste without compromising the unboxing experience. Your order arrives ready to give or to keep.

<Tip>
  The Galileo Set ships as a single package and makes an excellent gift as-is. No additional wrapping is needed.
</Tip>

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping notification with tracking information. Use the provided tracking link to follow your package's journey in real time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place your order">
    Complete checkout on the Galileo site. A confirmation appears on-screen immediately and is emailed to you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your shipping notification">
    When your order leaves our facility, you'll receive a second email containing your tracking number and a direct link to follow your shipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your package">
    Click the tracking link in your shipping email to see real-time carrier updates and an estimated delivery date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your order">
    Your order arrives in Galileo's signature minimal packaging, ready to enjoy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Questions About Your Shipment

If your tracking shows a delay, your package hasn't arrived within the estimated window, or you have any other shipping-related questions, contact us at support@galileo.com. Include your order number and we'll look into it right away.

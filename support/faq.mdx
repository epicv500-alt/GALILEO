Whether you're placing your first Galileo order or returning to expand your collection, you'll find answers to the most common questions below. If something isn't covered here, you can always reach us directly at support@galileo.com.

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="How do I place an order?">
    Browse the Galileo collection and select the product you'd like to purchase. Add it to your cart, then proceed to checkout. Enter your shipping and payment details, review your order summary, and confirm your purchase. Once your order is placed, a confirmation appears on-screen immediately and is also sent to the email address you provided at checkout.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is shipping really free?">
    Yes — every Galileo order ships complimentary, with no minimum purchase and no promo code required. There are no shipping fees added at checkout, ever.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?">
    We begin processing orders quickly to ensure prompt shipment, so changes and cancellations may not always be possible. If you need to modify or cancel your order, email support@galileo.com as soon as possible with your order number. We'll do everything we can to accommodate your request before the order ships. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you may be eligible to initiate a return after delivery.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does delivery take?">
    Estimated delivery times from the date your order ships are:

    - **Domestic (US):** 3–5 business days
    - **International:** 7–14 business days

    These are estimates and may vary based on your location, carrier availability, and seasonal demand. You'll receive a tracking link via email once your order ships so you can follow its progress in real time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't receive my confirmation email?">
    First, check your spam, junk, or promotions folder — confirmation emails occasionally land there. If you still can't find it, email us at support@galileo.com with your name and the email address used at checkout. We'll verify your order and resend the confirmation promptly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I return a product?">
    Yes. You may return any unused Galileo product in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery. To start a return, email support@galileo.com with your order number and the item(s) you'd like to return. Once we receive and inspect the return, your refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

    Please note that The Galileo Set must be returned as a complete set — individual components cannot be returned separately if purchased as part of the set.

    See our full [Returns and Refunds Policy](/support/returns) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are Galileo products suitable for sensitive skin?">
    The Bar and The Soak are formulated with a mindful approach to ingredients, and many customers with sensitive skin use them comfortably. However, everyone's skin is different. We recommend reviewing the full ingredient list for each product on its product page before purchasing, and performing a patch test if you have known sensitivities or allergies. If you have specific concerns, consult with a dermatologist or reach out to us at support@galileo.com.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What materials are The Mat and The Vessel made from?">
    Galileo designs The Mat and The Vessel with material quality and longevity in mind. For the most accurate and up-to-date material specifications — including fabric composition, construction details, and certifications — please refer to each product's page on the Galileo site. If you have a specific material question not answered there, contact us at support@galileo.com and we'll be happy to help.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I care for my Galileo products?">
    Care instructions vary by product and are included with each item at the time of delivery. You can also find care guidance on each product's page on the Galileo site. Following the recommended care instructions helps preserve the quality and appearance of your products over time. If you've misplaced your care card or have a question about a specific product, email us at support@galileo.com.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact Galileo?">
    The best way to reach us is by email at support@galileo.com. Include your order number in your message if your question relates to a specific purchase — this helps us respond quickly and accurately. We aim to reply to all inquiries within 1–2 business days.
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

Galileo keeps the post-purchase experience as clean as the shopping one. There is no account portal to log into, no dashboard to manage, and no loyalty program to track. Instead, everything you need about your order arrives directly in your inbox — and if something ever goes sideways, a real person is ready to help.

## Your Order Confirmation Email

As soon as you complete checkout, Galileo sends an order confirmation to the email address you provided. This email is your receipt and your primary record of the transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Look for an email from Galileo immediately after placing your order. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam or promotions folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the details">
    Review the items, quantities, shipping address, and order total listed in the confirmation. If anything looks incorrect, contact support right away — before the order ships.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the email">
    Your confirmation email is your proof of purchase. Keep it in a safe folder in case you need to reference it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Galileo does not currently offer an order tracking portal. Your confirmation email includes your order details, and the Galileo support team can provide status updates upon request.
</Note>

## Tracking Your Order

Once your order ships, Galileo will follow up with a shipping notification that includes carrier and tracking information. Use that information to monitor your delivery directly on the carrier's website.

<Tip>
  If your estimated delivery window has passed and your order has not arrived, reach out to Galileo support with your order confirmation email handy. The team can investigate quickly when you have your order details ready.
</Tip>

## Making Changes or Cancellations

If you need to change your shipping address, update your email, or cancel an order, act quickly — Galileo processes orders promptly.

<Warning>
  Changes and cancellations can only be accommodated before your order ships. Once an item has left the warehouse, Galileo is unable to redirect or recall it.
</Warning>

Contact support as soon as possible with:
- Your full name
- The email address used at checkout
- Your order details from the confirmation email

## Returns and Exchanges

Galileo stands behind every product it sells. If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, contact support within 14 days of delivery and the team will make it right. Include a photo of the item and your confirmation email when you reach out.

<Note>
  Galileo's return policy covers damaged or incorrect items. Because the collection is made in limited quantities, exchanges are subject to availability.
</Note>
